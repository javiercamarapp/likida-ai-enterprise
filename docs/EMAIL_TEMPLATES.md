## Email Sequence Strategy

### Timing
1. **Day 0:** Cold Outreach (Template 1)
2. **Day 3:** Follow-up (Template 2)
3. **Day 7:** Demo Invite (Template 3)
4. **Day 10:** Value Proposition (Template 4)
5. **Day 14:** Case Study (Template 5)

### Personalization Variables
- `{{nombre}}` — Contact's first name
- `{{empresa}}` — Company name
- `{{tu_nombre}}` — Your name
- `{{tu_cargo}}` — Your title

### A/B Testing Ideas
- Subject lines: Question vs. Statement
- CTA: "Agenda tu demo" vs. "Ve cómo funciona"
- Length: Short (3 sentences) vs. Medium (full template)
- Social proof: With testimonial vs. without

### Metrics to Track
- Open rate (target: 25%+)
- Click-through rate (target: 5%+)
- Reply rate (target: 3%+)
- Demo booking rate (target: 2%+)
