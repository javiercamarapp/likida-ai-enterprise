## Strategic Implications

### Strengths → Opportunities (S→O)
- **AI + accountant shortage** = Perfect timing for "virtual contadores"
- **Computer use + switching costs** = Bridge legacy systems during transition
- **Mexican expertise + regulatory changes** = First to comply with new rules
- **50+ integrations + growing market** = Comprehensive solution wins

### Weaknesses → Opportunities (W→O)
- **New brand + digital transformation** = Ride the wave; early movers get attention
- **Small team + partner ecosystem** = Partners extend reach without headcount
- **Limited track record + free pilots** = Generate proof points quickly

### Strengths → Threats (S→T)
- **AI differentiation + new AI competitors** = Stay 12 months ahead on innovation
- **Computer use + client inertia** = Solve the switching problem
- **Human-in-the-loop + trust deficit** = Prove safety and compliance

### Weaknesses → Threats (W→T)
- **New brand + established competitors** = Risk of being ignored or dismissed
- **Funding needs + price pressure** = Cash crunch if sales slow
- **Small team + technology changes** = Can't keep up with rapid AI evolution
