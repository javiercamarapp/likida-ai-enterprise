## Voice & Tone

### Brand Personality
- **Professional but approachable** — We are experts who speak human
- **Technical but not jargon-heavy** — We explain complex concepts simply
- **Confident but not arrogant** — We let results speak for themselves
- **Mexican Spanish (not Castilian)** — We speak the language of our market

### Language Guidelines

**DO:**
- Use "ustedes" form (not vosotros)
- Use Mexican terminology (e.g., "contabilidad" not "contable")
- Be direct and action-oriented
- Use short sentences and clear structure
- Include relevant Mexican fiscal context (RFC, SAT, CFDI, facturación)

**DON'T:**
- Use overly formal Castilian Spanish
- Use excessive acronyms without explanation
- Be condescending about technology adoption
- Make promises without backing them up with data

### Tone by Context

| Context           | Tone                          |
|-------------------|-------------------------------|
| Social Media      | Casual, engaging, insightful  |
| Blog Posts        | Educational, authoritative    |
| Email Outreach    | Direct, personalized, helpful |
| Product UI        | Clear, concise, guiding       |
| Sales Materials   | Professional, evidence-based  |

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
- Text: "Likida AI Enterprise"
- Color: `#4f8cff` on light backgrounds
- Minimum padding: 1x logo height on all sides

### Logo on Dark Backgrounds
- Use white (#FFFFFF) variant
- Maintain same padding rules

### Logo Don'ts
- Don't stretch or distort
- Don't change colors arbitrarily
- Don't place on busy/low-contrast backgrounds
- Don't add effects (shadows, outlines, gradients)

## Visual Style

### Imagery
- Clean, modern photography
- Mexican professionals in work environments
- Technology integrated naturally (not futuristic)
- Warm, inviting color grading

### UI Components
- Rounded corners (8px default)
- Subtle shadows for depth
- Blue primary buttons with white text
- Purple accent for highlights and badges
- Generous white space

### Icons
- Line style, 2px stroke
- Rounded caps and joins
- Blue or purple fill for emphasis

## Messaging Framework

### Value Proposition
> "Likida AI Enterprise automatiza la captura, procesamiento y conciliación de CFDIs para despachos contables mexicanos, eliminando errores manuales y ahorrando horas de trabajo repetitivo."

### Key Messages

1. **Problem:** Los despachos contables pierden horas capturando facturas manualmente
2. **Solution:** Inteligencia artificial que procesa CFDIs automáticamente
3. **Benefit:** Ahorra tiempo, reduce errores, cumple con el SAT sin estrés
4. **Proof:** Resultados medibles: 90% menos tiempo en captura, 99.8% precisión

### Tagline Variations
- Primary: "Tu despacho contable, corriendo en automático"
- Social: "Automatiza tu contabilidad con IA"
- Technical: "CFDI processing powered by AI"
