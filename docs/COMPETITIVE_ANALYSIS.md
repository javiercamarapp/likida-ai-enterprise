## 1. Market Overview

The Mexican accounting software market is dominated by established players serving contadores, despachos, and enterprise finance teams. Likida enters as the **first AI-native platform** for accounting automation in Mexico.

---

## 2. Direct Competitors

### 2.1 CONTPAQi (Market Leader)
- **Position:** Dominant player in Mexican accounting software
- **Strengths:** Deep SAT integration, brand recognition, extensive partner network, multi-decade track record
- **Weaknesses:** Legacy architecture, no AI capabilities, slow innovation cycle, complex UI
- **Pricing:** ~$5,000–$50,000 MXN/year depending on modules
- **Target:** Mid-to-large despachos and enterprise accounting departments
- **Threat Level:** HIGH — incumbent with switching costs and brand loyalty

### 2.2 Aspel (SAP-Backed)
- **Position:** Second-tier player, part of SAP ecosystem
- **Strengths:** SAP integration, strong in manufacturing/accounting overlap, modular pricing
- **Weaknesses:** No AI, less SAT-focused than CONTPAQi, limited automation
- **Pricing:** ~$3,000–$30,000 MXN/year
- **Target:** SMEs, manufacturing-adjacent accounting
- **Threat Level:** MEDIUM — smaller market share, but strong ecosystem

### 2.3 QuickBooks Mexico (Intuit)
- **Position:** Global brand, localized for Mexico
- **Strengths:** Brand trust, global best practices, decent SAT compliance, cloud-first
- **Weaknesses:** Limited AI, poor localization compared to CONTPAQi, expensive for Mexican market
- **Pricing:** ~$2,000–$20,000 MXN/year
- **Target:** SMEs, entrepreneurs, small despachos
- **Threat Level:** MEDIUM — global brand but weak in accounting enterprise segment

### 2.4 Xero Mexico
- **Position:** Global cloud accounting, emerging in Mexico
- **Strengths:** Cloud-native, modern UX, strong API ecosystem
- **Weaknesses:** Very limited Mexico presence, weak SAT compliance, no AI
- **Pricing:** ~$1,500–$15,000 MXN/year
- **Target:** Tech-forward SMEs, international businesses
- **Threat Level:** LOW — minimal Mexico traction

## 3. Indirect Competitors

### 3.1 Manual Accounting Processes
- **Prevalence:** ~40% of small despachos still use manual methods
- **Risk:** Low-tech but deeply embedded; switching inertia is real
- **Our advantage:** Cost savings and time reduction are obvious

### 3.2 Excel Spreadsheets
- **Prevalence:** Ubiquitous — even firms with software still use Excel for ad-hoc work
- **Risk:** Familiarity breeds comfort; Excel is "good enough" for many
- **Our advantage:** AI agents eliminate manual data entry entirely

### 3.3 Outsourced Accounting Firms
- **Prevalence:** Growing trend of outsourcing to cheaper regions
- **Risk:** Price competition from nearshore/offshore providers
- **Our advantage:** AI + human-in-the-loop beats pure outsourcing on quality and speed

## 5. Competitive Differentiation (Likida Moat)

### 5.1 AI-Powered Automation (UNIQUE)
No competitor offers AI agents that autonomously:
- Classify transactions
- Generate journal entries
- Reconcile accounts
- Prepare fiscal reports

This is a **category-creating** feature in the Mexican market.

### 5.2 Computer Use for ERPs (UNIQUE)
Likida's computer-use capability allows AI agents to interact with legacy ERP desktops (CONTPAQi, Aspel, SAP Business One) directly — no API needed. This bridges the gap between old and new systems.

### 5.3 50+ Integrations (MOST COMPREHENSIVE)
While competitors offer limited integrations, Likida's marketplace provides:
- Banking (10+ banks)
- E-commerce (Shopify, Mercado Libre, Amazon)
- Payroll (ADP, Nomina32)
- SAT (direct CFDI processing)
- CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce)
- ERP connectors

### 5.4 Mexican Fiscal Expertise (BUILT-IN)
Unlike global players (QuickBooks, Xero), Likida was built from day one for:
- CFDI 4.0 compliance
- SAT portal automation
- Monthly/annual fiscal obligations
- Retenciones, DIOT, Anexo 24

### 5.5 Human-in-the-Loop (TRUST)
Critical differentiator for regulated industries:
- AI proposes, human approves
- Full audit trail
- Explainability built into every decision
- Compliance officers stay in control

## 7. Win/Loss Strategies

### When Competing Against CONTPAQi
- **Win:** Lead with AI automation ROI (80% time savings), modern UX, faster onboarding
- **Risk:** Client has 10+ years of data in CONTPAQi; migration is a barrier
- **Mitigation:** Computer-use feature bridges both systems during transition

### When Competing Against QuickBooks
- **Win:** Superior SAT compliance, multi-client management for despachos, AI agents
- **Risk:** QuickBooks is cheaper and familiar
- **Mitigation:** Show TCO comparison including manual labor costs

### When Competing Against "We Use Excel"
- **Win:** Time savings (40+ hours/month per contado), error reduction, scalability
- **Risk:** "We've always done it this way" inertia
- **Mitigation:** Free pilot program; show ROI in first 30 days
