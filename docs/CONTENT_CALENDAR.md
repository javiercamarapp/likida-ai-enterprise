## Content Production Notes

### Writing Guidelines
- All content in Mexican Spanish
- Use "ustedes" form
- Include relevant Mexican fiscal terms (SAT, RFC, CFDI)
- Keep paragraphs short (3-4 sentences max)
- Use bullet points for scanability
- Include data/metrics whenever possible

### Visual Assets Needed
- Product screenshots
- Before/after infographics
- Client success graphics
- ROI calculator visualization
- Social media graphics (1200x627 for LinkedIn)

- [ ] LinkedIn: Schedule posts for 8:00 AM CST
