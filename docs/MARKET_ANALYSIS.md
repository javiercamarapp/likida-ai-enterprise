## 1. Market Sizing

### TAM (Total Addressable Market)
- **554 empresas contables** registered in Mexico (SAT data, 2025)
- Represents the entire universe of accounting firms and enterprise finance departments
- Market value: ~$2.5B MXN annually in software/services spend

### SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)
- **200 despachos with 5+ contadores**
- These are the mid-to-large firms that:
  - Have budget for technology investment
  - Handle complex multi-client portfolios
  - Are most affected by staffing challenges
  - Are most likely to adopt AI automation
- Market value: ~$800M MXN annually

### SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
- **50 despachos in first year**
- Realistic target based on:
  - Sales capacity (2-3 enterprise reps)
  - Onboarding timeline (2-4 weeks per client)
  - Pilot-to-contract conversion (30%)
  - Market awareness build-up
- Revenue target: $4M–$12M MXN in Year 1

## 2. Market Trends

### 2.1 Digital Transformation in Accounting
- 62% of Mexican despachos plan to increase tech spending in 2026 (encuesta CONTPAQi)
- Cloud adoption accelerating post-COVID
- Remote work driving demand for cloud-native solutions
- **Implication:** Tailwinds for Likida's cloud-first, AI-native approach

### 2.2 AI Adoption in Financial Services
- Global AI in accounting market growing at 30% CAGR (2024-2030)
- Mexico lags US/EU by 2-3 years in AI adoption
- **Implication:** First-mover advantage is real and time-limited

### 2.3 Regulatory Compliance Automation
- SAT is increasing digital reporting requirements
- CFDI 4.0 mandatory compliance since 2022
- New tax reforms (2024-2025) add complexity
- **Implication:** Compliance burden is increasing; AI automation becomes essential

### 2.4 Remote Work & Cloud Adoption
- 45% of despachos now support hybrid work
- Cloud accounting tools grew 35% in adoption (2023-2025)
- **Implication:** Cloud-native platforms have structural advantage

### 2.5 Talent Shortage
- 689+ vacant contadores positions across Mexico
- Average contado salary rising 15% year-over-year
- Burnout and turnover increasing in despachos
- **Implication:** AI augmentation (not replacement) is the only scalable solution

## 3. Growth Drivers

### Primary Drivers

1. **Accountant Shortage (689+ vacancies)**
   - Desperately need automation to do more with less
   - AI agents as "virtual contadores" is the pitch

2. **Increasing Fiscal Complexity**
   - New tax reforms, retenciones, DIOT requirements
   - More rules = more manual work = more AI opportunity

3. **Cost Pressure on Despachos**
   - Rising salaries, overhead, compliance costs
   - AI reduces cost per client by 40-60%

4. **Technology Adoption Acceleration**
   - COVID permanently shifted attitudes toward digital tools
   - Younger contadores expect modern UX

### Secondary Drivers

5. **SAT Digital Push**
   - Government mandating digital compliance
   - Creates urgency for automation

6. **Competitive Pressure**
   - Firms that adopt AI win more clients
   - Laggards lose market share

7. **Data Explosion**
   - More transactions, more complexity
   - AI is the only way to process at scale

## 4. Barriers to Entry

### For Likida (Challenges)
- **Brand recognition:** New player against decades-old brands
- **Switching costs:** Clients have years of data in legacy systems
- **Trust deficit:** AI in regulated finance requires proof
- **Regulatory complexity:** SAT compliance is non-trivial

### For Competitors (Advantages to Overcome)
- **Incumbency:** CONTPAQi has deep relationships
- **Data moat:** Legacy systems have years of workflow data
- **Partner networks:** Established reseller/integrator channels

### How Likida Overcomes Barriers
- Computer-use bridges legacy systems during transition
- Free pilot programs reduce risk
- Human-in-the-loop builds trust
- SAT expertise demonstrated in product
